# Persona 07 — casual research/work, round-two debate

Read all eight original reports (`01-gamer.md` through `08-office.md`) and `ballot.md`. These are simulated design judgments, not human usability findings. This round writes only my vote and debate; it does not change or run the app, a server, or a model. Native integration remains deferred.

## Specific challenges

1. **Gamer G02 and Tinkerer proposal 2: retain two equally available Beginner dials.** Their direct-adjustment goal is valid, but I disagree that prompt batch needs peer prominence for someone coming to summarize a document. Exact entry and keyboard operation remain available in Expert and Adjust. A visible Automatic/Custom summary addresses the discovery cost without creating a second immediate technical decision. I vote **B02 CONTEXT**.

2. **Mac M05: one selected recommendation and Agentic no-fit only when selected.** I prefer to see whether the three priorities produce different exact choices before interacting with a selector. The screenshot's problem is duplicated winners and large cards, not three priorities existing. Tinkerer proposal 5 and Office #6 supply a better compromise: one card per exact winner, profile chips, one action, and a compact visible no-fit row. I vote **B06 UNIQUE**, challenging M05's SELECTOR presentation. This avoids both duplicate reading and extra selector exploration.

3. **Gamer G05 and Tinkerer proposal 5: both rates on normal recommendation cards.** Those numbers help compare performance, but they cannot answer whether research outputs are useful or dependable. For an ordinary model commitment, estimated fit, working memory, download, exact identity and actual review status deserve the initial surface. I vote **B08 FIT** and **B19 VISIBLE**. This does not invent a research-quality measure: the review line must describe the actual metadata and its limits. Both rates remain in comparison and accessible Details.

4. **Teacher T06: “Show other file formats (quantizations).”** I reject this simplification because it supplies an inaccurate conceptual explanation. A shorter label such as All variants can retain the exact quantization names and disclosure explanation without calling quantization a file format. B07's exact readable identity and B10's preserved hierarchy address this need.

## Concessions and changed views

**Newcomer N02 has a useful opposing point about demo speed emphasis.** The large illustrative figures can draw attention away from actual fit. I nevertheless vote **B03 RATES**, holding my original Engine proposal 2: reducing metric size, putting fit first, and removing the timing path/taxonomy gives immediate adjustment feedback with less reading. Opening speed details on every adjustment is a real interaction cost. The rates' source must stay locally visible; compact speed is not a claim of total duration or reliable answers.

**Tinkerer proposal 5 and Office #6 changed my recommendation layout preference.** Round one proposed compact individual profile cards with shared-winner notes. I now support **UNIQUE** cards because simultaneous exact choices survive while repeated actions disappear. Distinct rationale and configuration identity must remain inspectable.

**Mac M10 and Newcomer N10 changed my setup structure preference.** My original report shortened all five existing steps. B12 OPTIONAL explicitly retains the download/configuration review, tuning access, preparation, resume and successful-readiness gate. I now favor **OPTIONAL**: a required visit named Tune suggests optimization is needed even when defaults suffice. Invalid retained values still need visible intervention.

**Teacher T04's concern about explanation and recovery is useful.** I originally proposed per-row Details in Expert. B05's Show descriptions gives someone investigating several settings a single action to expose explanations, avoiding one click per row. I support B05 while keeping scope, preserved-only status, errors and revert visible.

**Parallel proposals 1 and 4 add evidence outside my original emphasis.** Shared engine actions affect ongoing work, and the reported differing lifecycle guards justify consistent concise scope and blocker checks. B17 earns YES without adding idle counts, inferred concurrency or a new scheduling feature.

## Held positions and priorities

I hold the context-first Beginner hierarchy, exact variant access, qualified unknowns, honest cancellation consequences and a clear Ready finish. I also hold that speed, fit, model size and Balanced rank cannot establish research accuracy. B19 VISIBLE is one short actual review-status line replacing generic prose, not a new universal research checklist.

The card and Engine choices deliberately differ: Engine provides compact feedback for tuning (**B03 RATES**), ordinary recommendations lead with commitment and suitability evidence (**B08 FIT**), and comparison exposes both rates (**B11 YES**). The cost of speed disclosure is justified on a model card when it leaves space for facts that govern the decision; it is less justified while adjusting an Engine control repeatedly.

My top three are **B02, B06 and B11**, targeting the two-dial hierarchy, repeated winners and nested comparison panels. All twenty votes are explicit in `07-research-vote.json`. Amendments record wording/evidence refinements separately and do not condition any vote. Claimed usability benefits remain hypotheses for real-user validation.
