# Round 2 — teacher debate and ballot rationale

I read all eight independent reports and the common ballot. This remains a simulated older teacher's judgement, not observed human behavior. My criterion is whether shorter screens still let a capable PC user learning local AI predict the consequences and find help. No application work or execution was performed.

## Challenges to precise peer proposals

1. **02-mac M05: one selected recommendation with Agentic no-fit appearing only when selected.** That reduces the visible model objects, but hides a useful constraint until someone explores a priority. I prefer **B06 UNIQUE**: show one card per exact winner with profile chips and a compact no-approved-fit row. The row can explain why tools are unavailable without consuming a full-height empty card. The explicit compact no-fit requirement in B06 is stronger than M05's conditional visibility. Keep different configurations distinct even when a filename matches.

2. **01-gamer G02: keep both equally available dials with terse batch hint.** “Prompt processing batch · may use more memory” still asks a new user to interpret a technical adjustment. Learning it is possible, but it need not be mandatory before making the understandable context choice. I vote **B02 CONTEXT**, with Automatic or the exact custom batch visible and a labelled Adjust disclosure. This challenges the default hierarchy, not the gamer's access to batch tuning; Expert and direct values remain available.

3. **05-tinkerer proposal 12: Ready shows batch/cache/placement/threads in its loaded summary.** This repeats technical information at the point where the user is ready to begin a lesson-planning conversation. Accurate loaded configuration matters, but that accuracy can be preserved in Setup details. I support **B16** with exact model, profile/context and truthful readiness visible; complete successful-load configuration goes one disclosure deeper. A disclosure must show the loaded configuration, not simply the saved draft.

4. **04-newcomer N10: hide context/batch numbers and title the review Use recommended settings.** An easy default is useful, but custom retained settings make this title misleading. My preference is Review your setup, showing context and a Custom summary when retained, with optional adjustment. B14's explicit review and invalid-control expansion are better than making every review sound recommended.

## Useful opposing points and changed positions

**I concede 05-tinkerer proposal 3 and 07-research proposal 3 on Expert descriptions.** My T04 kept effects visible while proposing to hide recommended values on unchanged rows. Expert users need the recommended baseline for comparison; full effect paragraphs can be optional when applicability and unique limitations remain visible. I changed to **B05 YES**: keep recommendations visible and use a reversible Show descriptions control. This is a real change from round one, not a claim of agreement on every Expert detail.

**I also concede 06-parallel proposals 1 and 4 on global scope and lifecycle consistency.** My original review emphasized the individual's draft and recovery. A teacher may have several lesson chats open, so restarting a shared engine needs a concise global scope and known activity blockers. I support **B17 YES** without requesting a scheduling or concurrency feature.

**I correct two of my own phrases after 08-office proposal 7 and the source sequence.** My T06's “file formats (quantizations)” was inaccurate: say file variants, retain exact quantization codes and define the term in help. T09's “Downloads start on the next step” was too early; the next Tune step reviews settings, and its preparation action starts the operation. Say “Downloads start after reviewing your setup.”

## Positions held

- Keep **five named setup steps**. Tune is already optional within a distinct review stage. B12 FIVE preserves a clear commitment point and resume orientation; shorter content is enough without changing the step structure.
- Keep ordinary **Engine reading/answer ranges visible** with modest emphasis and source, fit and memory meaning. I reject B03 MEMORY for my persona: tuning should show its expected performance consequence without an additional search for the speed panel. This is a preference to validate, not a measured benefit.
- Keep only the **primary recommendation rate** by default. Recommendation selection and Engine exploration are different tasks; B08 PRIMARY reduces ordinary card reading while comparison still provides both rates.
- Keep existing profile names with short ordinary descriptors (**B13 KEEP**) rather than the offered rename. My round-one preferred label was Tools & long prompts; the offered Tools and agents still contains unfamiliar terminology and loses the long-prompt cue. I am choosing the better offered option, not recording a conditional rename vote.
- Keep one visible actual capability-review status (**B19 VISIBLE**), plus Demo/provisional/unknown/fit blockers. The office and research reviewers reinforce why throughput cannot stand in for task evidence.

## Priorities and limits

My top three remain **B04** (saving/restarting and recovery), **B14** (exact setup and download review), and **B15** (cancellation and retry consequences). The submitted JSON records one allowed choice for every B01–B20. Amendments are wording refinements, not conditional votes. No reductions in text size, icon-only actions, unsupported benchmark claims or hidden decision blockers are endorsed. Recommendations still require real user testing.
