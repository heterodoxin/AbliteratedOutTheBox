## Specific challenges

**02 Mac, M05: one profile selector with one recommendation.** I agree that three tall cards are wasteful, but selector navigation removes a useful comparison: whether different priorities actually select different exact variants. My round-one proposal 5 and B06 UNIQUE provide one card per real winner, profile chips and compact no-fit recovery. If all priorities choose different models, those are three meaningful objects. A selector works well in onboarding, where the user makes one choice, but Models also serves repeat inventory/comparison work. I vote UNIQUE, not SELECTOR; reducing object count does not automatically improve that task.

**04 Newcomer, N02 and N07: hide Engine rates and comparison reading throughput.** Fit must remain explicit and demo rates should lose their oversized hero treatment, but removing reading throughput conceals the bottleneck that matters when I feed a long prompt to a model. Answer throughput alone cannot substitute for it. Two normal-size, labeled ranges with source and compact memory are useful; the three timing stages and memory taxonomy are the repetition. I vote RATES for B03 and YES for B11's both-rate comparison. This does not turn speed into answer quality or task ETA.

**03 Teacher, T04: keep full effect paragraphs in Expert and disclose unchanged recommendations.** The outcome/timing of a parameter is important, but label/key/value/scope/recommended value/error/revert already form the working row. A full effect paragraph on every row makes a large changed-values scan harder. B05's Show descriptions gives a deliberate learning view without repeated reading in routine tuning. I vote YES. I retain T04's distinction between Revert to saved and recommended reset, and agree with T10/T11 that download and cancellation consequences belong beside the commitment.

**08 Office, proposal 7: “Show N file variants.”** I prefer the precise quantization term in the library because variants can differ in ways other than quantization. Friendly language should not make technical distinctions less clear. Exact names can remain readable with a short explanation in Details. This is a wording disagreement, not a request for more visible mechanism prose.

## Held positions and ballot reasoning

I retain unique exact-winner cards (B06 UNIQUE), both compact card rates (B08 BOTH), stable Agentic/Balanced/Performance labels with short descriptors (B13 KEEP), and the main save/apply/discard distinction (B04 YES). A primary rate alone saves very little space compared with removing duplicate purposes, nicknames and complete preview panels. A unique-card layout makes both rates particularly useful when one winner carries multiple profile chips.

I support hiding derived timing, memory taxonomy, illustrative tests and general explanations. None is automatically useful because it is technical. Conversely, exact identities, fit blockers, configuration scope, evidence source and review gaps affect a concrete decision and must remain legible. B18's single scoped source badge is sufficient when the metric group truly shares a source; it is not permission to hide Demo or treat estimated memory as measured speed.

Top three are B01, B05 and B06: remove repeated introductions, make Expert/Raw a concise working draft editor, and compact Models around unique exact choices.
