# Persona 02 — polished defaults, easy actions

Independent round 1. This is a simulated goal-based review, not a user study or evidence that Mac users share these preferences. My goals: start a useful chat with a sensible model, understand whether a change will interrupt work, adjust document capacity when needed, and reach every technical setting deliberately. I expect the app to choose a sound starting point and give me one clear next action.

## Inspected evidence

Read `engine-models-onboarding-2026-10-05.md`, the relevant identity/material/focus/navigation sections of `design-system-notes.md` and `interaction-guidelines.md`, and actual `Tuning.tsx`, `Models.tsx`, `Setup.tsx`, `EngineExperience.tsx` and `ModelCatalog.tsx`. Viewed screenshots 01 Engine light, 07 Models light, 09 onboarding profile, 08 comparison, 04 Engine mobile and 10 onboarding mobile. Current source/screenshots/report take precedence over historical design-system notes. No interaction, model execution, server or browser launch occurred.

The current materials, GT Walsheim, Anron symbols, green accent and quiet opaque reading surfaces already support this goal. The obstacle is simultaneous explanation: Engine presents two large instruments, multiple headings, speeds, durations, memory taxonomy, instructions and six footer actions. Models repeats the same winner in two full cards; the profile step repeats the selected profile's purpose below the profile choices. On mobile, screenfuls pass before the consequential action. Simplification should reduce visible objects and words without reducing truth or expert access.

## Must-do and opposed

My three must-do changes are **M01** (Beginner shows one meaningful adjustment), **M05** (one selected recommendation without duplicate winners), and **M07** (comparison is an aligned compact metric view). These address the most visible density while retaining precise variant identity and a deliberate advanced path.

I oppose **removing Expert/Raw or exact quantization identity to appear friendly**: ordinary defaults and complete expert access are compatible; filenames and settings must remain inspectable and reversible. I also oppose **replacing all provenance/fit/demo information with a generic green “Works great” badge**: this prototype cannot honestly assert native readiness or measured performance, and uncertainty should remain adjacent to the relevant decision.

No proposal calls for new decorative motion, glass on cards, gradients, extra shadows, different typography or a new identity. Retain the existing opaque surfaces and restrained control materials. Simplification is information hierarchy and disclosure, with errors, consequential actions and demo truth kept visible.
